# Ethics Statement

> [!CAUTION]
> **Ethics Banner:** This tool supports grading decisions. It does not replace expert judgment and must never be used to monitor or penalize workers.

## Responsible AI
The Explainable Livestock Health Grading System (ELHGS) is committed to responsible AI practices. We prioritize transparency, fairness, and human oversight. 

## Human-in-the-Loop
This system is an **assistive technology**, not an autonomous decision-maker. 
- **The human reviewer always has the final authority.**
- Every prediction generated by the AI must display its confidence score.
- Every prediction must be accompanied by a plain-text explanation of the deciding factors.
- Users can override the AI by submitting a disagreement for senior review.

## Privacy & Non-Surveillance Policy
To protect workers and maintain ethical operations:
- **No surveillance:** The application must not record audio or track employee location.
- **No employee ranking:** Grading metrics cannot be tied to individual employee performance scores.
- **No automatic punishment:** Disputes and errors are used to improve the system, never to penalize staff.
- **No facial recognition:** The system will not employ any form of human or animal facial recognition.
- **No animal identity tracking:** Animals are graded statelessly. The system will not track individual animal identities over time.

## Data Minimization
- No personal metadata or personally identifiable information (PII) is included in training datasets.
- All images must have their EXIF metadata (e.g., GPS coordinates, timestamps, device info) stripped before processing or storage.

## Limitations
The system inherits the biases of its training data. If partner farms historically under-graded certain health conditions, the AI may initially replicate this bias. Continuous human oversight is required to catch and correct these issues.
